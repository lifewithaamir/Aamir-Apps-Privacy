WhatsWeb Pro Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 27, 2026 · Last updated: November 27, 2026

Who we are

WhatsWeb Pro is an independent Android app developed by CH Aamir Habib. It displays WhatsApp Web in GeckoView and provides optional local utilities. WhatsWeb Pro is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by WhatsApp or Meta.

Information handled by WhatsApp Web

When you use WhatsApp Web, the website and WhatsApp/Meta handle your sign-in, messages, calls, and other activity under their own terms and privacy policy. That information is sent to the WhatsApp service through the website; WhatsWeb Pro does not operate a server that receives your WhatsApp messages.

Website cookies and local site data are kept in the app's on-device GeckoView profile so the website can maintain your session. Use Features → Log out / clear web session to clear that website data from this app. Clearing it means you will need to scan the website's QR code again. See the WhatsApp Privacy Policy for how WhatsApp handles information on its service.

Optional notification history

Notification saving is off by default. If you turn it on and grant Android Notification Access, the app reads notifications posted by WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business. It stores the notification title, preview text, and time in an encrypted database on your device. The app does not upload notification contents to a WhatsWeb Pro server.

You can turn off saving, revoke Notification Access in Android settings, clear all saved notifications, or select a 7-, 30-, or 90-day automatic retention period. Turning the feature off stops new entries but does not erase saved entries; use Clear all history to delete them.

Advertising and Google services

When ads are enabled and permitted by your privacy choices, WhatsWeb Pro uses Google's Mobile Ads SDK and consent tools. Google's SDK may collect and share information such as the device's IP address (which can be used to estimate approximate location), ad or app interactions, diagnostic information, and device or advertising identifiers. Google describes these uses as advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. Data handled by Google's SDK is transmitted using TLS, according to Google's published disclosures.

The app uses Google's consent flow where required and provides privacy options where Google requires them. Ads may appear in WhatsWeb Pro utility screens, but are not placed over or inside WhatsApp Web. Google's handling is governed by its own policies and settings: Google Privacy Policy · Google Mobile Ads data disclosure.

Permissions and files

  • Internet access loads WhatsApp Web and, when allowed, requests ads. Network-state access supports connection status.
  • Camera and microphone are requested only when the web page asks for them, such as for a call.
  • Location is requested only if WhatsApp Web asks to share your location and you choose to allow it.
  • Files are selected through Android's system picker. The app does not request broad storage access; a file is shared with WhatsApp Web only when you choose it there.
  • Notification Access is optional and is used only for the notification-history feature described above.

Information stored on your device

App preferences and the optional four-digit app-lock PIN are stored locally. The PIN is stored as a salted hash, not as readable PIN text. Android handles biometric verification; WhatsWeb Pro does not receive your biometric data. Notification history is encrypted using a key protected by Android Keystore.

WhatsWeb Pro does not create its own user account or operate its own analytics service. It does not send your web-session contents or saved notification text to a developer-operated server. Third-party services described in this policy may receive information needed for their own services.

Retention and deletion

  • Clear the WhatsApp Web session from the app's Features screen to remove its local website data.
  • Clear notification history at any time, or let the selected 7-, 30-, or 90-day retention setting remove older entries.
  • App preferences and local data can be removed by clearing or uninstalling the app, subject to Android's device behavior.
  • WhatsApp account information is managed by WhatsApp, not by WhatsWeb Pro. Use WhatsApp's own account controls for requests relating to that account.
  • Google advertising data is handled under Google's policies and retention settings; see Google's privacy links above.

Privacy questions

For privacy questions about WhatsWeb Pro, use the developer contact method shown on the WhatsWeb Pro Google Play listing. Please do not post private messages, phone numbers, or other sensitive information publicly.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when app behavior, permissions, advertising settings, or applicable requirements change. The latest version and its update date will remain available on this page.

This policy describes WhatsWeb Pro only. It does not replace WhatsApp's, Google's, or other third-party providers' own privacy terms.
